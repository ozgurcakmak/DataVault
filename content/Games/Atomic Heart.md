I have never thought I'd be longing for a future that never happened. 

Atomic heart gave me these vibes. What it does is giving a bright, pastel coloured communist utopia with sinister undertones. Yes, the robots have alleviated the labor - but they also have ==redacted==. 

## Notes
#### 07/08/2026
- As far as I can see, the game is biased towards glove powers + melee. Guns are also good but the bullet economy is pain. Energy based weapons leech off a renewable source but nah. Electricity + Ice Storm + Zvezdochka = Mega pain. AK-47/Pistol/Shotty = not so much pain
- Bots regenerate. Good for farming stuff; bad for temper. 
- Flying bots are annoying as mosquitoes. 
- Scratch that, **ALL** bots are annoying. They have cute designs, mostly, but they are annoying.
- In this universe, people pay to do the dirty with robot ballerinas. No, I do not know how. No, I do not want to know how. 
- Ivy monster - whatever its name (~~pluchy I think~~ It was Plyusch) - is a proper boss fight at first; when it becomes a regular monster - it is tiring.  The QTE part especially gets humdrum. Yesyes q, e, space... just get on with it so I can smack you more with my double glazed Zvezdochka.
#### 07/17/2026
Took 9 days but I've finally finished the game. Artwork is superb, scenario is good except the ending. It falls super flat at the end. There are DLCs but after finishing the main game, I didn't want to go any further. There is also the fact that you practically start from the beginning[^1]. 

All in all, it was a good 18h. The final bits - thanks to a fully upgraded Zvezdochka - was more in line of Kharn the Berserker vibe: Smash it until it stops moving. The gunplay was meh, someone wrote a thing in eksi which I fully agree: The hits you inflict on the enemy is portrayed well, the hits you receive... not so much. I always found myself checking the health gauge for seeing how badly I need a medikit.

Speaking of which, I was worrying about the resources but at the end the game gave me so much crafting ingredients - it wasn't funny. I, by myself, could shoulder a small war with the resources I had in my inventory. 

## Overall:
4/5 - Was good, I enjoyed myself and saw a dream set in the AH universe. Removed 1 pts for the scenario weakness at the end. 

[^1]:  passive upgrades come with you but the rest, you have to grind it again.
